VitalOps

Field operations for healthcare teams

VitalOps is a private field-operations app for invited staff, provided by Texoma Healthcare Group. Regional and field teams use it to plan and log site visits to the facilities they support, keep facility information in one place, track tasks and follow-ups, and message their team.

What the app does

  • Site visits with check-in/check-out, visit reports, notes and photos
  • Facility directory and history
  • Tasks and follow-ups across the team
  • Team chat and notifications
  • Sign in with email and password or with Google, plus Face ID on iPhone

Who can use it

Accounts are created by an organization's administrator; there is no public sign-up. When you sign in with Google, VitalOps uses only your name, email address and profile picture to sign you in to your existing account. See the Privacy Policy.
